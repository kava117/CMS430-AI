WRITEUP
=============================

The results of my initial exploratory analyses were successful, and revealed
that both tenure and contract type had effect over the retention of customers,
displayed in the plot_tenure_churn and plot_contract_churn .png files.

I decided to use two models, Random Forest and Logistic Regression, so that
I could compare the results of both. This serves the dual purpose of 
1) verifying that the models get similar results and 2) allows me to use the
model that is better suited to this problem. The confusion matrices of both
models have been saved in plot_cm_logistic_regression and plot_cm_random_forest.
Additionally, the classifications of each model have been visualized in the
plot_lr_odds_ratio and plot_feature_importance .pngs.

Using the predictions from the Random Forest, I would recommend that the company
begin new campaigns/incentives to retain long-time customers, and for
customers to increase their contract lengths. Additionally, from the Regression
model, I would recommend that when the company recruits new customers they focus
on those with no Internet service and avoid households that use fiber.
